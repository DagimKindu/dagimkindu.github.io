About Me

Turning Data Into Impact

I'm a Health Informatics graduate from the University of Gondar (CGPA 3.76/4.00) with hands-on experience across health information systems, research, and large-scale data collection. My work spans Ethiopia's public health sector, agricultural transformation, and development research.

I specialize in managing field and institutional datasets, performing data quality assurance, building survey tools, and generating reports that support decision-making. I've worked with DHIS2, HMIS, KoboToolbox, ODK, Survey Solutions, and analytical tools like Python, R, Stata, SPSS, SQL, and Power BI.

Beyond technical skills, I bring strong communication, teamwork, and problem-solving abilities — collaborating with field teams, partners, and stakeholders to ensure data is timely, accurate, and useful.
